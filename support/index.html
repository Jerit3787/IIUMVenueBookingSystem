Frequently Asked Questions

To book a venue, follow these steps:

  1. Click "New Booking" from the Bookings page
  2. Select your preferred venue from the available options
  3. Choose your date and time slot
  4. Fill in event details and upload required documents
  5. Submit your booking for approval

Required documents typically include:

  • Letter of approval from your department/organization
  • Event proposal or program details
  • List of participants (for large events)
  • Insurance documentation (if required)

The approval process typically takes 3-5 business days. For urgent requests or large events, please contact our support team directly.

Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking:

  • Pending bookings: Can be modified or cancelled anytime
  • Confirmed bookings: Must be cancelled at least 48 hours in advance
  • Modifications: Subject to venue availability

Our venue capacities are:

  • Main Auditorium: 500-850 participants
  • Banquet Hall: 350 participants
  • Experimental Hall: 150-260 participants
  • Mini Auditorium: 50-140 participants
  • Multi Purpose Rooms: 40-100 participants

Booking fees vary by venue and event type:

  • IIUM Events: Free for university-sponsored events
  • Student Organizations: Subsidized rates available
  • External Events: Commercial rates apply

Contact our support team for detailed pricing information.
