About IIUM Venue Booking System

Streamlining venue reservations for the IIUM community

Our Mission

The IIUM Venue Booking System is designed to provide a seamless, efficient, and transparent venue reservation process for students, faculty, staff, and external organizations at the International Islamic University Malaysia. Our goal is to maximize venue utilization while ensuring fair access to all university facilities.

Booking Process

Select Venue

Browse available venues and choose based on your event requirements and expected attendance.

2

Approval & Confirmation

Your booking will be reviewed and you'll receive confirmation via email once approved.

Booking Policies

Advance Booking

All venue bookings must be submitted at least 7 days in advance for regular events and 14 days for major events requiring special setup.

Cancellation Policy

Bookings can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the event without penalty. Late cancellations may be subject to charges.

Documentation Requirements

All bookings require proper documentation including event proposals and departmental approval letters.

Usage Guidelines

Venues must be used in accordance with university policies. Damage to facilities will result in repair charges.
